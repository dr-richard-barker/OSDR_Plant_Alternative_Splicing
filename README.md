# Differential Alternative Splicing in Plant Spaceflight Transcriptomes

A coordinated multi-study alternative splicing (AS) analysis of plant spaceflight transcriptomes. It covers 201 samples from 8 studies in the NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR): 199 samples from 7 *Arabidopsis thaliana* studies and 2 samples from 1 *Brassica rapa* study (OSD-59), spanning microgravity and partial gravity on the ISS, simulated cosmic radiation, and Apollo lunar regolith. All results below come from the 7 *Arabidopsis* studies. The OSD-59 outputs and the analysis code are not yet in this repository.

## Key Scientific Findings

1. **Retained Introns:** Retained intron (RI) events are the largest class of significant events in 6 of the 7 *Arabidopsis* studies (51–72%). The exception is OSD-37, where only 10 events were significant (3 RI).
2. **Heuristic NMD class:** 79–86% of significant events are classified as potentially NMD-sensitive by a frameshift heuristic that counts every RI event as NMD-sensitive. This share largely reflects the RI fraction. NMD itself was not measured.
3. **Circadian GO terms:** Circadian-related GO terms are enriched in four of the five ISS flight datasets: OSD-314 (FDR 0.032), OSD-120 (FDR 0.097), OSD-37 (FDR 0.091, one gene, *RVE1*) and OSD-678 (FDR 0.008). They are not enriched in OSD-251. Each term rests on 1–4 genes. OSD-120 (root tips) and OSD-678 (leaves) come from the same CARA flight.
4. **Stressor-Specific Enrichment:**
   - Apollo lunar regolith (OSD-476, 306 events): the top terms include regulation of photosynthesis light reactions and of the mitotic cell cycle (FDR 0.138 each).
   - Simulated GCR radiation (OSD-658): phospholipid metabolic process is enriched (FDR 0.013).
   - Fractional gravity (OSD-251): response to superoxide is enriched (FDR 0.001).
5. **Most recurrent gene (AT4G17310):** Splicing of *AT4G17310* (an uncharacterized protein) is significantly altered in 4 of the 7 studies: fractional gravity, altered gravity, lunar regolith and GCR radiation. The event type and direction differ between studies (RI in two, SE in two). The gene is not significant in the three ISS flight-versus-ground datasets (OSD-120, OSD-37, OSD-678).
6. **Dominant Batch Effects:** Machine learning on the 199-sample × 9,875-gene PSI matrix shows that study-specific batch effects dominate the splicing profiles:
   - UMAP ARI is 0.995 for study and −0.002 for treatment.
   - Leave-one-study-out AUC is 0.49 (RF), 0.42 (GBM) and 0.41 (XGBoost).
   - Pooled nested 5-fold AUC is 0.598 ± 0.084.
